Inner Orbit

Real constellations, made playable

Pick something to try.

Two small prototypes built on real star and constellation data. Both run in your browser, and neither needs an account.

  • New · prototype

    Star Launcher

    Aim a ball through a real constellation and light every star in as few launches as you can. Fill a constellation to learn about it.

    Play Star Launcher →

  • Earlier prototype

    Memory Sky Wallpaper

    Pick a place, a date, and a time to see the real sky above that moment, then save it as an iPhone wallpaper.

    Open Memory Sky Wallpaper →

Privacy: Star Launcher makes no network requests beyond this site's own files. Memory Sky Wallpaper sends only the place name you search for to Open-Meteo; your title, date, and time stay in your browser.

Star and constellation data come from d3-celestial. Credits and licenses are in vendor/THIRD_PARTY.md.
